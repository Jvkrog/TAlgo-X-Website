One decision path, not a hardcoded indicator.

Most bots wire an indicator straight to an order. TAlgo-X puts a dedicated decision layer in between — OneBrain reads the scanner's read on the market and the strategy's intent, and only then does anything reach execution.

//Market Scanner

Scan before you commit capital.

TAlgo-X classifies every configured instrument into a market regime before OneBrain ever sees it. Only instruments in a tradeable state get passed downstream — everything else sits out.
